# Design Updates Outline

Summary: The main design direction for VoxCTL is to shift the app away from showing abstract usage stats as the primary experience and toward showing the user's actual transcriptions first. The homepage should become a transcription-focused workspace inspired by WhisperFlow's directness and Superwhisper's simplicity, with stats moved into a dedicated page. The recording cards and files/history experience should be dramatically simplified so entries are readable, plain, searchable, easy to copy, and only reveal deeper metadata or actions when expanded. Overall, the app should feel cleaner, less noisy, and more focused on the core value of voice-to-text.
